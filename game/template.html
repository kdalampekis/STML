Wugs and Daxes Not started

Find out what must be true about things you have never seen.

Each world is full of made-up kinds. You get a few true statements about them. Your job is to work out what else follows, and only what follows.

  1. The premises are true. Every kind has at least one member.
  2. Propose a statement only if it must be true given the premises, not just if it could be.
  3. Before each answer, rate how sure you are. You can also tap the premises you used and say why.
  4. A critic replies. When you are wrong it first asks you a question; tap Show why to see the full answer.
  5. After each world comes a short test: 4 statements to judge as must be true, must be false, can't tell from the premises, or I don't know yet. An honest "I don't know yet" scores better than a wrong answer.
  6. Then write a sentence about what you noticed. There are 5 levels and 3 worlds per level.

Example world

P1All wugs are daxes
P2All daxes are feps

Does All wugs are feps follow? Does All feps are wugs? The critic will tell you, and show you a world that proves it.

Your moves, confidence ratings and notes are recorded to study how people learn logic. Use a code, not your name.
